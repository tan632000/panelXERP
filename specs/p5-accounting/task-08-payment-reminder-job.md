# Task 08 — Daily payment reminder job and its notices

Status: pending

## Outcome
Every day at 07:00 Vietnam time (and once at start-up) the system notifies the accountant role, and the right owner, about milestones and supplier invoices that are due soon or overdue, once per subject, stage and due date; settled, re-dated and cancelled subjects stop being reminded and their open notices close.

## Scope
- In (D-15, D-16, D-17, D-13, D-14): feature `payment-reminder` (no permissions, no endpoints) with `PaymentReminderJob` (shape of `overdue.job.ts:12-47`: `@Cron('0 7 * * *', { timeZone: 'Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh' })`, one run in `onApplicationBootstrap` not awaited, awaited in `beforeApplicationShutdown`, a failed pass logged and never raised), `PaymentReminderService.run(today: string)` returning counts and logging `Payment reminder check for <today>: <n> candidates, <m> notified, <f> failed` (counts only, as `overdue.service.ts:100-102`), and `PaymentReminderNotifier` (emit and close of the four event types). Constants: `REMINDER_WINDOW_DAYS = 3`; events `accounting.entry.voided` and `accounting.receivable.reminder`, `accounting.receivable.reminder.owner`, `accounting.payable.reminder`, `accounting.payable.reminder.owner`; dedupe keys `milestone:<id>:<stage>:<dueDate>` and `supplier-invoice:<id>:<stage>:<dueDate>`; `stage` ∈ `due_soon|overdue`. Candidates are read without a lock; each is decided again in its own transaction holding its accounting row `FOR SHARE` (never an order or PO lock, `overdue.service.ts:111-121`), then: milestone candidate = `amount − Σ live collections > 0` and its order not `cancelled` (live status through `OrderMaterialReadPort.getOrders`); supplier-invoice candidate = live, `amount − Σ live payments > 0`, `dueDate` not null and its PO not `cancelled` (through `PurchaseOrderFinancePort.getHeaders`). Stage: `overdue` iff `dueDate < today`; `due_soon` iff `today ≤ dueDate ≤ today + 3`; otherwise none. Raise per audience when no open record exists (`findOpenByDedupeKey`): role broadcast `accountant` (`requiredScope` `receivable:read` / `payable:read`) and the owner by name (`userIds`, scope `receivable:read` for the order's current owner from `OrderMaterialReadPort.getOrder`; `purchase_order:read` for the PO's `createdBy`, payload exactly the D-16 key sets, never an amount, links per D-16); raising `overdue` closes that subject's `due_soon` records (both audiences) in the same transaction; unique violation `23505` = already raised (`overdue.service.ts:55-58,90-92`); a subject whose order/PO is `cancelled` has its open records closed by the run. Writers close open records of a subject in their own transaction (both stages, both audiences, the current due date): a collection or payment that brings `toCollect`/`due` to 0; a milestone PATCH that changes `dueDate` (old date) or lowers the amount to what is already collected; a soft-deleted milestone; a supplier invoice voided while it has no live payment (R-04). Void notice (D-22, user F-06): the four void services (customer invoice, collection, supplier invoice, payment) call `PaymentReminderNotifier.voided(...)` after their commit: one `accounting.entry.voided` role broadcast to `director`, dedupe key `void:<kind>:<id>`, payload exactly the D-22 key set, `requiredScope` per kind, never an amount, a failed emit logged and never raised. An order or PO that the status hydrate does not return is skipped and its id logged (F-04); a soft-deleted milestone is never a candidate. `ReceivableModule` and `PayableModule` import the reminder module for `PaymentReminderNotifier`; `accounting.features.ts` registers the feature.
- Out: bell wording (task-09); a daily repeat (Q5); reminders to the director by name (kernel role-broadcast branch, `notification.service.ts:443-446`); email.

## Steps
1. Job → `payment-reminder.job.spec.ts` (fake clock 2026-11-02T23:30Z): start-up run calls the service once with `2026-11-03` (Vietnam date, not UTC); cron metadata equals `0 7 * * *` with `timeZone: 'Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh'` (`SCHEDULE_CRON_OPTIONS`); a rejected run is logged and shutdown resolves; start-up does not block listening.
2. Stages → `payment-reminder.service.spec.ts` (real Postgres, `today` passed in): milestone due today → `due_soon`; due yesterday → `overdue`; due today + 3 → `due_soon`; today + 4 → nothing; fully collected → nothing; a supplier invoice without `dueDate` → nothing; voided invoice → nothing; cancelled order and cancelled PO → nothing and their open records are closed.
3. Dedupe and recipients: two runs on one day, and the next day, leave exactly one open record per subject, stage, due date and audience; a due-soon notice followed by the overdue day yields one overdue record and the due-soon one closed; two concurrent runs → one record per audience (unique violation absorbed); receivable records: role `accountant` with empty `userIds`, owner record with `userIds = [current owner]` and empty roles — the owner after a reassignment is the new one; payable records: role `accountant` and `userIds = [createdBy]` with `requiredScope = purchase_order:read`; every payload's key set equals the D-16 contract exactly (so a payable payload holds no `amount`, `due`, `invoiced` or `payable` key); a director reads the role records and not the owner records (`NotificationService.listForActor`); the accountant reads its own record; a technician reads neither.
4. Clearing in writers: a collection that settles a milestone closes its open records (assert status `resolved`); a partial collection leaves them open; a milestone PATCH to another due date closes the old-date records and the next run raises a new key; a PATCH that lowers the amount to what is collected settles it and closes the records; a soft-deleted milestone's records close and it is never raised again; a supplier invoice voided with no payment closes its records; a payment that settles an invoice closes its records; voiding the settling collection reopens eligibility (the next run raises again).
5. Failure: a subject whose notice emit throws is counted failed, the run continues and the others are raised; the log holds counts and ids only (no amounts).
6. Live start-up run: after `docker compose restart backend`, the log shows the start-up pass line once with candidate/notified/failed counts (recorded in the receipt).
7. Void notice (AC-17) → `payment-reminder.service.spec.ts` and the four writer specs: voiding a customer invoice, a collection, a supplier invoice and a payment each raises exactly one `accounting.entry.voided` record (role `director`, no user ids, `createdBy` = the voiding accountant, dedupe key `void:<kind>:<id>`, `requiredScope` `receivable:read` or `payable:read`) whose payload key set equals the D-22 contract and holds no `amount`; the director reads it through `listForActor`, the accountant, sales and an engineer do not; a void by the director raises none visible to them; an emit that throws leaves the void committed and logs once.
8. Races (the first marked [UNVERIFIED until run], R-03): job ∥ collection that settles the milestone, both orders (`queuedBehind`, holder holds the milestone row): collection first → no open notice remains on the settled milestone; job first → the notice it raised is closed by the collection; never an open notice on a settled subject; same for a payment settling a supplier invoice.
9. Defensive (F-04): an order whose request row is soft-deleted (so the hydrate omits it) is skipped without error and logged; a soft-deleted milestone with `toCollect > 0` is skipped.
