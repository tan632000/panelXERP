# Director full access, order follower notices, and prices hidden from engineering
Specs-Contract: process-first-ready-v1

## Scope decision (GATE-SCOPE — 2026-11-04)
- Request (vi): giám đốc full quyền, full thông báo, edit mọi thứ; giám đốc tạo đơn thì duyệt luôn và thông báo các phòng liên quan; mỗi khi phòng nào sửa/cập nhật đơn hàng thì thông báo các phòng liên quan; phòng kỹ thuật không được biết giá; kỹ thuật nhập xong số lượng thiết bị thì chuyển sales điền giá; giá chỉ sales và giám đốc thấy/sửa.
- Existing: role grants in `backend/src/kernel/core/database/seeds/seed-roles.ts:72-107` (director without estimate writes, `:72-76`); notifications are role- or user-addressed records (`notification.service.ts:13-34,353-387`), 13 emit call sites; four-eyes rules at `quotation-approval.service.ts:392-405`, `purchase-order-approval.service.ts:228-250`, `stocktake-approval.service.ts:273`; order edits send nothing (`order-edit.service.ts:43-133`); the item price rule is `reference_price:read` (`item-price-visibility.util.ts:7-23`); estimate writes are limited by role code (`estimate.service.ts:57-58,443-462`).
- Minimum change: the five decisions below. Expansion signals: about 60 touched files after GATE-REVIEW (harness, e2e and spec owners added), two new services (`OrderFollowerNotifier`, `EstimatePricingService`), one migration; one packet because every outcome shares the role grants of task-01.
- User decision: KEEP, with these decisions:
  1. Director becomes `*:*` like admin (system users/roles included), receives every business notification (refined at GATE-REVIEW R-03: personal second-person notices to another named user are excluded), may write estimates; every explicit `admin` role-code site is audited (D-01, D-02).
  2. A quotation version, purchase order or stocktake submitted by the director is approved at once; four-eyes is skipped for the director only; the approved notice reaches the usual audience plus the departments that act next (D-03, D-04).
  3. Every edit/update of a sales order notifies salesOwner, salesManager, director, purchasing, storekeeper and productionManager except the actor: contract/delivery edit, cancel, production release/cancel/complete, stock issue (D-05).
  4. engineer and engineeringLead lose every price: `reference_price:read` removed, money keys omitted from their responses, price UI hidden, item create/import by them never sets a price; purchasing keeps its price scopes (D-06).
  5. Engineers enter panels, BOM items and quantities and "Gửi dự toán"; sales, salesManager, director (and admin) fill unit costs and other costs (default = reference price) and mark the estimate priced; a quotation needs a priced estimate (D-07, D-08).

## Out of scope
- Notices for quotation, purchase order or production order edits beyond decision 3.
- P4 work; any doc rewrite beyond regenerating `docs/permission/role-matrix.md` section 3 (no generator script exists: the procedure is `role-matrix.md:150`) and the estimate/approval steps of `docs/user-flow-guide.md`.
- Admin as an estimate writer or as exempt from four-eyes (admin keeps today's behavior).
- Re-opening a priced estimate without a new revision (D-08 break signal).
- Merging duplicate records in the director's bell (D-01 break signal).
